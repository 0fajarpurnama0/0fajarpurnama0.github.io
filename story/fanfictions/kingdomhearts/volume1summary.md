## Fajar Purnama Biography

Based on the "Fajar Purnama Infinite Realities Biography", Fajar's journey from his second life onward transforms him from a rejected outcast into an omnipotent multiversal deity.

### The Tragic Origin & Immortality

Following his rejection from the elite Inner Circle of the Apex Society, Fajar forms the "Fiction to Reality" underground syndicate to unlock multiversal travel. While they successfully map supernatural zones, his comrades' extreme specialization leads to their slaughter during an underworld ambush. Left as the sole survivor and carrying immense guilt, Fajar binds his soul to a newly opened, infinite realm, achieving immortality and a resurrection mechanic to begin his endless wandering.

### The Cultivation Modules

His power scales systematically through two distinct training phases:

* **Phase I: Reality:** Focuses on forging the perfect biological vessel through primal movement, sensory overclocking, and a psychological "2nd Person View" to manually regulate his nervous system and detach from trauma.


* **Phase II: Fiction:** Breaks the limits of physics through internal energy cultivation (Qi/Chi), metaphysical reality domination, and the "3rd Person View" to impose his absolute will upon multiversal laws.



### The Escalation of Combat Realities

Fajar hones his vessel and combat instincts by plunging into increasingly hostile fictional realities:

* **Foundational Realms:** He begins in *Almost Zero Combat* worlds to master societal optimization and healing, then intentionally strips away his pristine techniques to learn dirty street fighting in *Brawl Realities* and flawless biomechanics in *Unarmed Combat Realities*.


* **The Vanguard Shift:** In *Paranormal Realities*, he combines physical force and Phase II heat to exorcise spirits with his bare fists. After a mass slaughter to protect innocents during a city riot, he accepts a dead-cold pragmatism and enters *Armed Combat Realities*—mastering tactical espionage, blades, and heavy ordnance in universes like *Metal Gear Solid* to break their canonical tragedies.


* **Super Power & Fate Breaking:** Acting as a multiversal anomaly, he drops into high-power worlds like *Fairy Tail* and *Gosu* to violently shatter tragic fates, protecting the innocent while comfortably shouldering the sins of his brutal methods.



### Ascension to Full Deity

To untie the final knot in his heart, Fajar returns to his original reality and uses gravity-distorting energy to completely obliterate the supernatural lords who killed his original syndicate. With his lingering trauma severed, he enters the highest cosmic universes. By mastering absolute will in *One Piece* and angelic neutrality in *Dragon Ball*, Fajar officially ascends as the Lord of Infinite Realities, setting the definitive stage for his Almighty Fan Fictions.

## Prologue

### The Multiversal Awakening and Core Premise

The narrative commences in the immediate aftermath of the catastrophic Keyblade War from *Kingdom Hearts Union χ*. As the devastating "Goodnight" echoes through the fading remnants of Daybreak Town, the Player’s distinct male and female avatars, alongside their loyal Chirithies, transition from a sterile sleep realm into the breathtaking cosmic expanse of Infinite Realities. Here, they are confronted by their true original consciousness: Fajar Purnama, the human player who has successfully ascended beyond the constraints of mortality to reign as the omnipotent Lord of Infinite Realities. In this supreme domain, Fajar reveals that the avatars are not merely disposable game protagonists, but fully actualized extensions of his own divine soul who are now granted complete autonomy, immortality, and a grander purpose beyond the Master of Masters' predetermined tragedy.

### The Avatars: Aura and Raja

Awakening as distinct, free-willed individuals, the avatars adopt names that serve as anagrams of their creator: Aura, the female operative, and Raja, the male vanguard. Visually striking, they don the iconic Organization XIII-style black coats accented with vivid crimson scarves. While Aura adorns her hair with a vibrant red hibiscus flower, Raja commands an imposing, unmasked presence, allowing his stoic features to complement his overwhelming martial aura. Philosophically, they operate as absolute pragmatists who completely reject the restrictive dogma of Light versus Darkness, willingly wielding dark powers simply for their tactical efficiency and aesthetic dominance while maintaining genuinely heroic hearts dedicated to saving the innocent. Their combat synergy is tethered by a fledgling "Hive Mind," a nascent psychic link requiring intense synchronization of a shared third-person perspective to perfectly mirror each other's devastating strikes. Raja embodies the "Apex," an immovable, stoic pillar of pure resilience and lethality drawing upon the overpowering presence of Heavenly Demons, Madara Uchiha, and Gol D. Roger. Conversely, Aura embodies the "Weaver," utilizing her emotional vulnerability, female perspective, and profound empathy to forge genuine bonds with key figures like Ephemer, Skuld, and Ava—achieving diplomatic victories that pure strength cannot secure.

### The Day One Insertion and Shadow Operations

Rejecting a simple regression to the eve of the Keyblade War, Aura and Raja choose to execute their temporal dive back to "Day One"—the precise moment they first obtained their Keyblades. They deliberately bypass the ancient Dive to the Heart's restrictive binary choices, actively refusing to surrender any potential and opting to rely entirely on their own biological and spiritual vessels rather than traditional magic. Operating as untraceable anomalies outside the Book of Prophecies, their overarching mission is not to prematurely break the timeline, but to manipulate it from the shadows. By adopting the terrifying persona of rogue, villainous combatants, they plan to systematically "attack" their own allies, secretly evacuating these supposed casualties to the Infinite Realities checkpoint to preserve a foundation of survivors for the future. Throughout this extensive timeline, they will patiently allow the Foretellers to succumb to True Darkness before ruthlessly enlightening them through overwhelming physical force.

### Power Cultivation and The Divine Descent

Fajar structures their growth meticulously, starting them with novice magical parameters but injecting complete Tier 1 Bio-Mechanics and Tier 2 Sensory Overclocking directly into their nervous systems to ensure absolute baseline lethality. Their progression mandates the mastery of two distinct modules. Phase I: Reality demands the forging of a perfect human vessel through brutal close-quarters combat, joint locks, sensory mastery, and primal movement. Phase II: Fiction dictates supernatural ascension, combining Ki cultivation, reality domination, and dynamic form-changing Keyblades that shift into gauntlets or firearms based purely on imagination. Should they encounter a catastrophic threat beyond their cultivated limits, Fajar retains the ultimate trump card known as "The Descent". This allows the Lord of Infinite Realities to temporarily possess their bodies, unleashing terrifying God of Destruction-level power and absolute Angelic neutrality upon the battlefield, albeit at a massive, agonizing physical toll to the avatars' mortal vessels once his divine presence departs.

### Narrative and Revision Analysis

Chapter 1 operationalizes the foundational parameters established by the Lord of Infinite Realities, Fajar Purnama. Rather than accepting the preordained tragic narrative of *Kingdom Hearts Union χ*, the autonomous avatars Aura and Raja systematically dismantle the canonical script from within. The narrative shifts from standard magical progression to an emphasis on physical mastery, tactical pragmatism, and biological optimization.

```
Canonical Keyblade Wielder                The Unbound Avatars (Aura & Raja)
```

2. **Subversion of Canonical Entities:** Forces both the Foretellers and Luxu to confront unscripted variables, breaking the Book of Prophecies' predictive authority.


3. **Philosophical Realignment:** Re-educates key figures—specifically Ephemer, Skuld, and Master Ava—redirecting the future Dandelion Union Leaders away from blind obedience and toward critical self-determination.

* **Tier 1 (Foundation & Maintenance):** Full-body joint stretching, primal movements, crawling, and grounding defenses.


* **Tier 2 (Instinct & Awareness):** Sensory Overclocking (isolating fountain splashes and bakery scents), Environmental Awareness (evaluating cobblestones as projectiles and fountain rims as bludgeons), Neuro-Somatic Cultivation, and Second-Person View.


* **Tier 3 (Combat Basics):** Shadowboxing combining primal instincts with MMA mechanics, improvised weapon stances, and execution of The Art of Invisibility.


* **Tier 4 (Specialist Training):** Identification of anatomical "Go Muscles," dynamic flexibility parkour wall-runs, close-quarters combat (CQC) joint locks, and downloading the Library of Motion into their nervous systems.




* **Weapon Unlocking:** Recalling how past wielders relied too heavily on magic and static weapons, they unlock their Keyblades' geometry. Aura shifts hers into a sleek combat knife, while Raja configures his into a CQC shield/blade hybrid.

* **Evolved Darkside:** The Heartless compresses into a 10-foot-tall, bone-armored humanoid resembling an Arrancar, complete with a localized heart, individual ego, and a bone-forged Keyblade.


* **Foreteller Stalemate:** Master Aced, Invi, and Gula engage the beast, but its newly granted intelligence allows it to parry strikes and redirect magic, catching the Foretellers off guard.

## Chapter 2 Part 1

* Ephemer, Skuld, Aura, and a blindfolded Raja travel from Daybreak Town to the Castle of Dreams, which the Chirithies explain is a physical projection of the future.

* Unbeknownst to the main group—though sensed by Aura and Raja—Strelitzia secretly follows them through the portal and observes their journey from the shadows.



### Multiversal Training Interludes

As the party navigates the world, reality periodically shifts, providing them with critical combat upgrades:

* **Pragmatic Combat:** An alleyway transitions to a construction lot where the Eunjang High crew is fighting Union delinquents. Aura forbids the use of Keyblades, forcing Ephemer and Skuld to learn spatial awareness and environmental combat from Gray Yeon.


* **Ki Control (Qinggong):** While searching for a pink ribbon, the woods shift to a badlands where Gohan and Videl are training. The party learns to externalize Ki, allowing them to decouple their mass from gravity to achieve a weightless hovering technique.


* **Anatomical Hardening:** A morning jog transports them to a Shaolin Temple run by Muijin, who teaches them to brace their cores and isolate muscle groups. By flooding these tensed muscles with Ki, they achieve a Proto-Iron Body capable of effortlessly absorbing magical strikes and heavy physical blows.


* **Observation Haki:** The Tremaine woods morph into the prehistoric jungle of Rusukaina, where Rayleigh and Luffy teach them Kenbunshoku, or the Color of Observation. Ephemer and Skuld learn to sense the raw intent of wild beasts and the complex attacks of humans without using their eyes.



### Protecting the Timeline and Cinderella's Light

* The party helps Jaq and Gus secure dress materials, but when Lady Tremaine and her daughters destroy the finished gown, Raja forcefully stops Ephemer from intervening to preserve Cinderella's personal resilience and timeline.


* After the Fairy Godmother provides a magical carriage and a new dress, the party defends the carriage from a Malice Hound and later escorts Cinderella up the palace staircase, clearing Heartless without disrupting the royal event.


* Following the midnight waltz, the party watches Cinderella flee and lose her glass slipper.


* Later in the woods, Raja reveals his traumatic past to the group, explaining that he previously embraced the Darkness and isolated himself to save his former friends from bearing the burden. Aura comforts him, cementing their deep bond and leaving Ephemer and Skuld in awe of their friends' resilience.



### The Slipper and the Keyhole

* The party acts as the Grand Duke's bodyguard, using their new Iron Body techniques to effortlessly tank Large Body Heartless on the road to the Tremaine chateau.


* Upon arriving, they discover Lady Tremaine has locked Cinderella in the tower. As the mice drag the heavy iron key up the stairs, the party defends them against a swarm of Heartless and an Envious Matriarch boss.


* After Lady Tremaine shatters the glass slipper, Cinderella produces the matching shoe, which fits perfectly and summons the world's Keyhole.


* Ephemer and Skuld lock the Keyhole together, and after attending Cinderella's royal wedding, the party returns to Daybreak Town via a Corridor of Light to rest.

## Chapter Riot

The "Chapter Riot" anomaly detour diverges from the standard fairy-tale progression, plunging the party into a dark, modern urban conflict that explores the tragic origins of their multiversal creator. By contrasting a bloodless, magic-assisted pacification with the horrific reality of the original timeline, the chapter deepens the thematic weight of Fajar Purnama's absolute pragmatism and reinforces the profound emotional bonds between the Keyblade wielders.

### The Multiversal Ambush and Pacification

* A heavy gravitational anomaly tears through the Corridor of Light, pulling Aura, Raja, Ephemer, Skuld, and a hidden Strelitzia into a gritty, violent urban riot in another dimension.


* Landing in an alleyway, they encounter a serious high school boy attempting to protect a terrified girl from armed thugs.


* Ephemer, Skuld, and Strelitzia use magic to non-lethally neutralize the attackers, shocking the boy, whose demeanor instantly shifts to an enthusiastic and friendly pragmatism.


* The boy orchestrates a "Five-Minute Rule" strategy where Ephemer and Skuld cast massive magical distractions from the rooftops every five minutes to disorient the rioters.


* This sensory overload allows the ground team to sweep through the burning city, efficiently knocking out thousands of rioters without shedding a single drop of blood.



### The True Identity and the Ghost Spectators

* After successfully pacifying the capital and securing commendations, the boy formally introduces himself as Fajar Purnama, a reincarnator.


* This revelation completely paralyzes Aura and Raja with shock, as they realize they are interacting with the younger, mortal version of their omnipotent multiversal creator.


* While Ephemer, Skuld, and Strelitzia safely transition back to Daybreak Town, Aura and Raja are violently yanked back into the anomaly world as ethereal spectators.


* They witness the original timeline where they never intervened, watching a cold, ruthless Fajar brutally slaughter the thugs and over a thousand rioters alone using kitchen knives and lethal strikes to protect the innocent.


* Aura and Raja observe Fajar endure the crushing terror of the victims he saved and the suffocating weight of dark spiritual energy without faltering.



### The Burden of the Dawn

* Upon finally returning to Daybreak Town, Aura collapses and breaks down in tears, overwhelmed by profound empathy for Fajar's agonizingly dark and isolated path.


* Raja grimly explains Fajar's tragic reality to the rest of the party, revealing that the boy willingly absorbed the hatred of the world so innocent people wouldn't have to die.


* Strelitzia comforts Aura and Raja by emphasizing that their accidental intervention fundamentally changed his fate for one night, allowing Fajar to fight alongside allies and be a hero with clean hands.
