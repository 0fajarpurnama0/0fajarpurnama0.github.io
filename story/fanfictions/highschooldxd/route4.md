## Route 1 + 2 Epilogue 
### Part 1: Convergence in the Void

The Harrowing Interstice was a realm designed to shatter the mortal psyche. It was an endless, apocalyptic expanse of ashen wastelands beneath a sky that bled swirling violet nebulas. The atmospheric pressure was suffocating, heavy with the agonized, psychic wails of forgotten entities and the cosmic dust of dead universes. Yet, as Ren walked deeper into the absolute darkness, his boots crunching against the primordial ash, a deeply unsettling realization washed over him. He did not feel crushed. He did not feel terrified.

He felt entirely at home.

The pitch-black Qi circulating through his synchronized Dantians resonated with the ambient despair of the void. The Interstice was not rejecting him; it was wrapping around him like a heavy, familiar coat. For a mind that had discarded the illusions of mundane paradise in favor of absolute, pragmatic strength, this desolate wasteland was a sanctuary of pure focus.

Ren walked for what felt like hours, or perhaps days—time was a fractured concept here. He was lost in the meditative rhythm of his own internal engine when his universal senses picked up a drastic anomaly.

Footsteps. Heavy, rhythmic, and completely unbothered by the crushing gravity of the void.

Ren stopped, his dark eyes narrowing as a silhouette emerged from the violet mist. The figure was not a multi-dimensional horror or a lost soul. It was a teenage boy. He had an athletic, heavily muscled build that strained against the fabric of his clothes, a bright, fiercely determined expression, and an energetic gait that looked more suited for a morning jog than a trek through a nightmare dimension.

Most jarring of all, however, was his attire. The boy was wearing the exact same black-and-white Kuoh Academy uniform as Ren.

The two stopped a few yards apart, staring at one another in the dead center of the abyss.

"Whoa!" the boy exclaimed, his voice booming with a bright, pure-hearted energy that seemed entirely alien in the Interstice. "No way! You go to Kuoh Academy too? I thought I knew all the strong guys at school, but I've never seen you before! I'm Ariel. Ariel Caelum Veritas."

Ren analyzed the boy. Ariel radiated a simple, overwhelming aura of physical power, reminiscent of a shonen protagonist—a muscle-brained warrior who lived for the thrill of the fight and the grind of the training arc. He was the antithesis of Ren’s cold, tactical pragmatism, yet here he was, surviving the void on sheer physical vitality.

"Ren," he replied smoothly, uncrossing his arms. "And yes, I am a student at Kuoh. The fact that we have not crossed paths is statistically improbable given our mutual tolerance for this environment. How exactly did you end up in the Harrowing Interstice?"

Ariel laughed, rubbing the back of his head with a sheepish, wide grin. "Man, it's a long story. But hey, you're here too! How did you pull it off? I mean, Kuoh is weird, but it ain't 'walk through a tear in spacetime' weird for most people."

"My trajectory was... complicated," Ren admitted, deciding that sharing intelligence was the most logical first step. As they began to walk side-by-side through the wasteland, Ren laid out his history.

He explained his mundane beginnings as a member of Kuoh's infamous 'perverted quartet,' a life defined by shallow, degenerate desires. He spoke of the turning point: the evening at the park when Raynare struck.

"Issei was killed, and Rias Gremory came to resurrect him," Ren explained, his voice clinical and detached. "But I was collateral damage. A stray blast from the Fallen Angel blew me deep into the treeline. I was bleeding, crippled, and forced to crawl through the dirt while Issei was granted salvation and inducted into a world of magic. The sheer isolation of that moment—the realization of my own absolute insignificance—planted the seed of this very dimension inside my soul."

Ren detailed how he completely discarded his harem dreams in the aftermath, realizing that survival required immense sacrifice. He began wearing a blindfold to school, subjecting himself to severe sensory deprivation to force his other senses to compensate.

"Wait, hold on!" Ariel interrupted, his eyes practically turning into stars. "You trained by walking around blindfolded to sense intent and spatial movement? Dude! That is literally Observation Haki!"

Ren blinked, momentarily derailed by the sheer enthusiasm of the comparison. "Observation Haki... from *One Piece*?"

"Yeah! The Color of Observation!" Ariel beamed. "That's exactly what it is!"

Ren let out a rare, amused chuckle. To Ren, it was the universal synchronization of Qi to detect kinetic shifts in reality. But to communicate with Ariel, using the anime lexicon was infinitely faster and surprisingly accurate. "Very well. Yes, I awakened my Observation Haki. I utilized it shortly after, when Raynare's subordinates came to kidnap Asia Argento. I was able to perfectly predict and dodge their light spears, countering with combat knives laced with holy water. Unfortunately, Issei lacked the same spatial awareness and was severely wounded."

"Classic Issei," Ariel snorted. "So what happened next?"

"The church raid," Ren continued. "I attempted to initiate a tactical, stealth-based espionage infiltration to minimize casualties. Issei, predictably, kicked the front doors wide open and alerted the entire compound. Rias and Akeno had not yet arrived, forcing us into a brutal, unequal engagement. I took on the entire human priest faction single-handedly so Issei and Kiba could focus on the Fallen Angels."

Ren's tone darkened slightly. "But when the battle ended and the dust settled, they left. They simply forgot I was there, buried in the rubble. That second abandonment was the catalyst. The seed of the Harrowing Interstice fully sprouted within me. I cleared my blocked Dantians and meridians, harmonizing the dark energy of this void with my own internal Qi, completely overhauling my martial arts."

"So you got a massive zenkai boost and leveled up your base stats," Ariel nodded sagely, completely tracking the logic.

"Precisely. I then began practicing kinetic density manipulation," Ren explained. "I learned to harden my skin and muscles to the density of steel to shatter holy swords on impact. Furthermore, I learned to project my internal will onto the physical plane, utilizing raw atmospheric pressure to crush my enemies' minds and bodies simultaneously."

Ariel grabbed Ren by the shoulders, shaking him in excitement. "Bro! Armament Haki! And Advanced Conqueror's Haki! You literally unlocked all three types!"

### Part 3: The Multiversal Nexus

The descent into the deeper strata of the Harrowing Interstice was a test of absolute biological and spiritual endurance. The ambient environment shifted from an ashen wasteland into a violent, hyper-pressurized storm of multiversal decay. The gravity here was non-linear, pulling at their organs from multiple dimensions simultaneously, while the atmospheric pressure carried the crushing weight of a collapsing star. A standard high-class Devil from the DxD universe would have been atomized within seconds of crossing the threshold.

Ariel marched forward, his body wreathed in a blinding, golden armor of Advanced Armament and Conqueror’s Haki, his muscles expanding to forcefully repel the crushing gravity. Beside him, Ren walked in absolute silence, his pitch-black Qi synchronized perfectly with the void, matching the frequency of the destruction to render himself virtually intangible to its effects.

"The concentration of dark energy is exponentially denser here," Ren noted, his voice slicing cleanly through the roaring winds of the abyss. He looked up at the swirling cosmic vortexes bleeding through the shattered sky. "Look at the currents, Ariel. They are not random. They are flowing backward."

Ariel squinted through the violent violet mists, his Observation Haki flaring. "You're right. It’s like looking at a bunch of massive rivers pouring into a single ocean."

"A logical inevitability," Ren analyzed, his eyes tracking the jagged, glowing fissures that began to appear in the fabric of the void around them. "If the Harrowing Interstice is the multiversal cesspool—the ultimate destination for the raw negativity, forgotten grievances, and shattered souls of countless timelines—then it serves as a cosmic nexus. By walking upstream against these primordial currents, we are essentially tracing the exhaust back to its engines. These fissures are the bleeding edges of other realities."

They had reached a junction in the void, a sprawling, impossible plateau where the sky fractured into countless shards of different realities. Some fissures bled the neon light of cyberpunk dystopias; others radiated the serene, suffocating mana of high-fantasy realms or the sterile cold of cosmic science fiction. The sheer influx of overlapping physics and conflicting universal laws made the plateau a zone of agonizing sensory overload.

Ariel suddenly stopped, his golden aura spiking violently. He turned toward a massive, spiraling rift that pulsed with a heavy, rhythmic heartbeat of pure, unadulterated power.

"There," Ariel breathed, his bright eyes wide with an instinctual, primal excitement. "Ren, do you feel that? It’s roaring. Whatever is on the other side of that rift... it’s challenging me. It’s calling me specifically."

Ren turned to analyze the rift, preparing to calculate the optimal entry vector. But as he stepped closer, his analytical mind completely flatlined.

The pitch-black Qi circulating through his three Dantians—the energy born from his brutal realization of abandonment, his ruthless pursuit of survival, and his embrace of the dark—suddenly shifted. It did not react defensively. It did not brace for combat. It softened, expanding with a profound, resonant warmth that Ren had never experienced in his life in Kuoh.

A deeply buried, fragmented memory sparked within his soul. It was a sensation completely divorced from the perverted high schooler he had pretended to be, and even distinct from the cold, pragmatic warrior he had become. It was the feeling of standing side-by-side with equals. It was the memory of a time before the multiversal cosmos had broken him in a forgotten war.

Ren closed his eyes, his breathing hitching. He did not feel the anxiety of stepping into the unknown. He felt the overwhelming, undeniable gravity of returning to a place he had been violently torn from eons ago.

"I feel it too," Ren whispered, his voice losing its usual clinical detachment, replaced by a quiet, absolute certainty. "I... I am close to home."

Ariel looked at Ren, sensing the massive shift in his comrade's spiritual signature. The muscle-brained warrior grinned, giving Ren a firm, grounding clap on the shoulder. "Then what are we waiting for? Let’s see what’s on the other side."

Side by side, the tactician and the brawler stepped through the spiraling tear in reality, leaving the apocalyptic storms of the Harrowing Interstice behind.

The transition was instantaneous and absolute. The suffocating, toxic ash and the wails of forgotten souls vanished, replaced by an atmosphere of pure, sovereign energy. They stepped out onto a grand platform of impossibly pristine white marble, suspended in an endless, breathtaking cosmos where the stars were woven together by hand into constellations of gold and silver light. The architecture of the realm was infinite, defying all conceptual limits of space and time—a domain built not by natural cosmic evolution, but forcefully forged by a singular, supreme will.

The air was heavy, but not with despair. It was heavy with majesty.

Standing at the edge of the grand platform, looking out over the infinite expanse of his self-made reality, was a lone figure. As Ren and Ariel fully materialized in the realm, the figure turned to face them.

He radiated a power that effortlessly dwarfed the Heavenly Dragons and the pantheons of Kuoh. He was a deity who had shattered his mortal limits, conquered the void, and built a sovereign domain from the ashes of a forgotten war. Yet, as he looked upon the tactician who had finally awakened from his healing slumber and the brawler who had fought his way through the multiverse to find him, the absolute force of his aura melted into a profoundly human, nostalgic smile.

Fajar Purnama spread his arms, his voice echoing with the warmth of a brotherhood that time, death, and the multiverse could not erase.

"Welcome back, my comrades," Fajar said softly. "My dear friends."

"We are human," Ren answered calmly, tilting his blindfolded head toward the Knight. "Nothing more. The issue is that modern humanity has fundamentally forgotten what that entails. Society today dismisses sensory honing, internal energy cultivation, and extreme biological conditioning as myth, choosing instead to rely solely on basic physical fitness and superficial fighting techniques. We simply pushed human martial arts and internal energy cultivation to their absolute, logical extremes."

"Exactly," Ariel chimed in, flexing a bicep. "Any human could technically achieve what we do if they trained hard enough. Those 'limiters' you heard us mention were just highly dense, internal training weights we placed on our own energy pathways to make daily life more challenging. Taking them off just let us access our full, cultivated human potential."

Rias and her peerage stared at them in profound disbelief. The idea that pure, unadulterated human martial arts could bridge the gap to the supernatural was fundamentally alien to them.

"Fascinating," Akeno murmured, her eyes glittering. "But that does not explain your knowledge. You knew Issei possessed the President's flyer, and you deliberately abandoned him to save your other friends because you knew we would resurrect him. How do you know so much about our operations?"

"Our sensory training isn't just physical," Ren explained smoothly. "We cultivate our awareness to sense intentions, biological rhythms, and non-human signatures. We were aware that Issei was carrying an anomalous magical artifact. We were also fully aware that your Rook, Koneko, was monitoring Issei for days."

Ren turned his head precisely toward where Koneko was sitting. "For example, she was sitting on the branch of the cherry blossom tree observing us when Issei was attempting to peek at the Kendo club last Thursday."

Koneko paused mid-bite of her chocolate bar, her golden eyes widening in genuine, uncharacteristic shock. A faint blush dusted her cheeks as she realized these "humans" had easily detected her espionage.

"As for our deeper knowledge of your world," Ren continued, his voice taking on a slightly more serious, rhythmic tone. "We possess a promise to fulfill regarding the Occult Research Club. It is a vow that binds us to your future. However, we cannot explain the exact parameters of this promise yet. We ask that you wait exactly one month. After the conclusion of the upcoming Rating Game incident—the Riser Phenex affair—we will reveal everything."

Rias flinched visibly. The mention of Riser Phenex—a deeply guarded, highly personal political nightmare she had kept entirely hidden from her peerage—proved beyond a shadow of a doubt that these brothers possessed information they had absolutely no right to know.

"The third boy," Koneko suddenly spoke up, her voice regaining its stoic edge. "The one who utilized Future Sight. Who is he? What is the 'Infinite Reality' he returned to?"

"Ah, Fajar," Ariel laughed warmly. "He's a comrade in arms. He's the founder of our little 'fiction to reality' community. We basically took concepts like internal cultivation, advanced haki, and dimensional physics that normal people dismiss as fiction, and proved they weren't myths. Fajar is currently in his own realm. Explaining the mechanics of his existence right now would just give you all a massive headache. Like Ren said, we ask for your patience until next month."

The room fell silent as Rias processed the overwhelming, paradigm-shifting information. These were not enemies. They were anomalous, impossibly strong humans who treated supernatural threats like sparring partners, yet they possessed a baffling, respectful loyalty to Issei, Matsuda, and Motohama.

Slowly, the icy, regal facade of the Crimson-Haired Ruin Princess melted away. Rias sighed, her shoulders relaxing as a genuinely warm, kind smile spread across her face. The imposing monarch vanished, replaced by a caring, deeply empathetic young woman.

"I want to make something perfectly clear to both of you," Rias said softly, looking between Ariel and Ren. "I hold absolutely no prejudice against humans. The Occult Research Club is a sanctuary, and I have no problem whatsoever with humans joining us or associating with my peerage. My hostility earlier was not born of arrogance."

Rias glanced toward the window, her expression tightening with genuine worry. "It was born of fear. The supernatural world is unimaginably dangerous. My primary duty as a King is to protect my servants. When humans get involved with us, they inevitably become targets. And frankly, baseline humans are fragile. They cannot protect themselves. If you join our circle, it endangers my peerage, because we would be forced to constantly divide our attention to keep you alive."

She turned her gaze back to them, her eyes full of sorrowful concern. "I am not worried about the two of you anymore. You have proven you can dismantle a Fallen Angel strike team with terrifying ease. But Matsuda and Motohama are truly, entirely mundane. They survived by sheer luck yesterday. If they continue to associate with Issei, they will be targeted again. And I cannot guarantee their safety."

Ariel’s grin softened into a look of absolute, unshakable resolve. He stepped forward, the iron weights clanking decisively.

"You don't have to guarantee their safety, President," Ariel said, his voice carrying the immovable weight of a mountain. "We will."

Ren adjusted his posture, his multiversal presence bleeding just slightly into the room—a warm, protective aura that felt fundamentally different from demonic magic. "Issei has the Gremory clan to protect him. We will take full, absolute responsibility for Matsuda and Motohama. If any supernatural entity, regardless of their faction, attempts to harm them, we will handle it personally. You do not need to divide your forces to protect our friends."

The heavy, lingering anxiety in the clubroom evaporated instantly. Rias’s eyes widened, touched by the sheer, unwavering loyalty these anomalously powerful humans possessed. Kiba smiled genuinely, recognizing the pure chivalry in their declaration, while Akeno let out a soft, approving hum.

"Very well," Rias said, her warm smile returning in full force. She extended her hand across the desk. "Ariel Veritas. Ren Veritas. We accept your terms, and we look forward to the revelations you promised next month. Until then, you are officially welcome in the Occult Research Club territory as esteemed friends."
